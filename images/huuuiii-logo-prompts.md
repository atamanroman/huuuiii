## Symbol prompt

Asset type: original standalone logo symbol for huuuiii, a small macOS utility that turns pointer motion into effortless scrolling while a modifier key is held.
Design a single highly refined, distinctive symbol: a flowing lowercase h that resolves into a subtle cursor-arrow tip, expressing fluid scrolling and a little playful speed.
The whole symbol should be one cohesive silhouette, not an h with a separate icon pasted onto it.
Think a bold broad rounded stroke, carefully balanced counters and negative space, one elegant directional turn, and an unmistakable small pointer-like terminal.
The character should feel quick, lighthearted, and precise, suitable for a well-crafted independent Mac utility.
Style: exceptionally clean flat vector-like graphic, one solid near-black ink color, crisp smooth edges, no outlines within the filled mark.
Composition: just one centered symbol, square canvas with generous transparent margin, symbol occupying roughly 65 percent of the canvas; no tile or enclosing shape.
Strong legibility and distinguishable silhouette when reduced to a 16-pixel menu bar icon.
No fine lines, no tiny detached details.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha, no checkerboard drawn into the image.
Constraints: original design, no text or wordmark, no slogan, no mockup, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no glass, no background, no watermark, no imitation of existing company logos.
Produce a polished finished logo symbol, not a concept sheet.
